# Product context and proposed validation

Evidence checked on 2026-10-03 using official product pages only. No account signup, customer outreach, paid service, institutional site access, or patent research was performed.

## Existing alternatives

- [MixerSeater: how it works](https://www.mixerseater.com/how_it_works): seating rotations, minimizing duplicate meetings, printable charts, individual agendas, and mobile agendas. Its [pricing/features page](https://www.mixerseater.com/price_list) also lists “don't sit with” and dynamic seating. These are established capabilities, not Round Loom inventions
- [Flippity Random Name Picker](https://flippity.net/v2/rp.php) and [official overview](https://www.flippity.net/): groups, teams, seating charts, rosters, speed networking, and printer-friendly controls
- [Sage Teachers Groupings](https://sageteachers.com/groupings): group generation, keep-apart/together rules, absences, recent-pair avoidance, mixed station rotations, and printing. The page describes a free account for saved classes. It is a classroom tool; Round Loom is intentionally limited to synthetic adult-workshop labels

The last alternative is a particularly close overlap. Do not pitch “avoid repeated groups,” “respect absences,” or “print rotations” as unique. This comparison does not establish that competitors lack local-only computation, frozen-history import/export, or independent validation; those properties were not comprehensively audited.

## Narrow hypothesis

For a facilitator of a small adult workshop, a no-account local planning file that preserves completed rounds and exposes a recomputable encounter matrix may be easier to inspect and hand off than editing a spreadsheet. Round Loom packages that workflow into a small prototype. Its value and willingness-to-pay are untested; existing free options may be sufficient.

## Proposed validation, requiring separate permission before outreach

Use synthetic 9-, 13-, and 24-label exercises. Ask an authorized test facilitator to:

1. Build a balanced initial plan
2. Freeze two rounds
3. Remove one participant only from future rounds
4. Check whether past tables changed
5. Print tables and personal routes, and locate a repeated pair

Compare task completion time, correction errors, and comprehension of “best found” against their existing spreadsheet or chosen existing product. Suggested prototype acceptance targets: no changed historical assignments, no omitted/duplicate attending labels, all hard keep-apart conditions satisfied, correct identification of search exhaustion, and understandable safe exports. These are proposed criteria, not measured customer results.

## Portfolio evidence

The technical demonstration is bounded optimization with an independent validation boundary, exact historical state preservation, deterministic reproduction, safe local imports/exports, and UI concurrency cancellation. A portfolio presentation should show both the successful synthetic examples and the unexecuted-browser verification gap until CI or a permitted browser run supplies actual evidence.
